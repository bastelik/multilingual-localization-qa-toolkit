# Multilingual Linguistic QA Checklist

Use this checklist as a final review aid. Not every item applies to every content type; tailor it to the brief and record any exclusions.

## Assignment and references

- [ ] Correct source file, version and target locale are being reviewed.
- [ ] Audience, purpose, channel and content type are understood.
- [ ] Current brief, glossary, style guide and reference materials are available.
- [ ] Open queries and approved exceptions are visible to the reviewer.

## Accuracy and completeness

- [ ] All source content requiring translation is represented.
- [ ] No unsupported claims, explanations or promises have been added.
- [ ] Actors, actions, objects, relationships and sequence are preserved.
- [ ] Negation, modality, conditions, warnings and uncertainty are preserved.
- [ ] Numbers, dates, units, names and factual details are correct.
- [ ] Meaning is appropriate in the full context, not just sentence by sentence.

## Language and terminology

- [ ] Grammar, syntax, spelling and punctuation are correct.
- [ ] Terminology follows the approved glossary and documented decisions.
- [ ] Register, tone and voice match the brief.
- [ ] Wording is natural and clear for the target audience.
- [ ] Locale conventions are followed consistently.
- [ ] Ambiguity has been resolved or explicitly escalated.

## Structure and technical integrity

- [ ] Headings, lists, tags, placeholders and variables are preserved.
- [ ] Links, references, labels and cross-references are correct.
- [ ] Text is not truncated and fits the intended interface or format.
- [ ] Formatting, line breaks and character restrictions are respected.
- [ ] Required metadata and file naming conventions are correct.

## Content-specific checks

### Subtitles and timed text
- [ ] Timing and synchronization are appropriate.
- [ ] Reading speed and line length meet project requirements.
- [ ] Line breaks support comprehension.
- [ ] Speaker identification and sound cues follow the specification.
- [ ] Captions preserve relevant meaning without inventing information.

### AI-generated or AI-assisted language
- [ ] Factual claims are checked against appropriate sources where required.
- [ ] The output follows all explicit instructions and constraints.
- [ ] Unsupported certainty, fabricated citations and missing caveats are flagged.
- [ ] Repeated outputs use terminology and style consistently.
- [ ] Sensitive or high-impact content receives the required human or expert review.

## Delivery

- [ ] All critical and major issues are resolved or formally escalated.
- [ ] Corrections have been rechecked in context.
- [ ] Review comments are clear, specific and evidence-based.
- [ ] Final file and version are identified correctly.
- [ ] Remaining limitations, assumptions and unresolved queries are documented.
